    Getting around off the rails

    China does not recognise the International Driving Permit. To self-drive you need a temporary Chinese driving permit (临时驾驶许可, valid up to 90 days). Get it from the local vehicle administration office, some of which have desks at major airports, with your passport, visa or entry stamp, German licence plus a certified Chinese translation, and photos. It is only valid for small cars (C1/C2). The rental companies (eHi, CAR Inc.) can help. The easier alternative is a car with driver for a few days (≈ ¥600–1,000/day, book via your guesthouse, Trip.com or Didi's charter option), or long-distance buses plus village minibuses.

    Festivals in the window

    🎉 Dai Water-Splashing Festival, Xishuangbanna, 13–15 April (fun, very crowded). 🎉 Miao Sisters' Meal Festival, Shidong/Taijiang (Guizhou), 15th–17th day of the 3rd lunar month, which is 21–23 April 2027 (official events often run longer).

    Avoid

    🚫 Qingming (≈ 5 Apr) and especially the Labour Day holiday (1–5 May): domestic travel peaks, trains sell out, top sights get overwhelmed.
